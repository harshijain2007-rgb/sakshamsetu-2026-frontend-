Terms of Service & Accessibility Commitments

Enacted pursuant to the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act 2016 and Harmonised Guidelines 2021.

1. Statutory Obligation & Scope

SakshamSetu provides accessible digital, physical, and academic services to ensure universal barrier-free participation for students with disabilities across the campus ecosystem. All services comply with mandatory government directives.

2. Equal Examination & Academic Accommodations

Enrolled students are entitled to reasonable accommodations including qualified scribes, compensatory examination time (minimum 20 minutes per hour), accessible e-Text/braille materials, and assistive workstation access.

3. Non-Discrimination & Anonymous Grievance Right

Every campus member has the unconditional right to file barrier reports anonymously. The administration is bound by law to assess and remediate reported obstructions without requiring student identity disclosure.

4. Accessible Transit & Emergency Protocol

Campus electric shuttle vans and medical facilities are priority-reserved for students with mobility impairments and acute medical needs. Emergency triggers dispatch immediate response teams without administrative hindrance.
